About

I am a doctoral candidate and research assistant in Digital Engineering and Construction at the Institute of Technology and Management in Construction (TMB), Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), supervised by Prof. Reza Maalek. My work sits at the intersection of Building Information Modeling (BIM), structural engineering, and artificial intelligence.

My research combines BIM and visual programming (Dynamo) with finite element analysis, metaheuristic optimization, reliability analysis, and sustainability assessment. Building on this, I develop Sketch-to-BIM workflows that turn hand sketches, speech, and text into parametric, optimized BIM models, and Scan-to-BIM methods that align point clouds and panoramic imagery with BIM for semantic reconstruction. I have also worked on fusing augmented reality with ultra-wideband positioning for construction applications.

Before joining KIT as a research assistant in 2024, I completed a Master's degree in Civil Engineering at Karadeniz Technical University, with thesis research carried out at KIT, and a Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering at Sakarya University. Outside research, I build open-source Python and web applications for learning and productivity.

Research

My doctoral research addresses AI-based optimization of design and construction-process management in BIM environments, supervised by Prof. Reza Maalek at KIT TMB. The foundation is a BIM-based structural optimization framework in which Dynamo visual programming drives parametric model generation, finite element analysis in Robot Structural Analysis, metaheuristic optimization, reliability analysis (First-Order Reliability Method), and life cycle assessment within a single workflow.

On top of this framework I develop Sketch-to-BIM, which extracts features from hand sketches, and more recently from speech and text, to automatically generate and optimize BIM models. Two complementary lines extend the work toward the built environment: Scan-to-BIM, covering automatic point-cloud-to-BIM alignment and semantic 3D reconstruction from registered point clouds and 360-degree panoramas, and AR-UWB sensor fusion for more stable positioning in construction applications.
